Two notes on getting this file onto disk, both kept because they are the interesting part:
the counts section was first written empty - the log lines carry a GitHub timestamp prefix and
the filter meant to drop noise was `grep -vE "^20.."`, which deleted exactly the three lines
wanted while the section header survived and the script exited 0; and the truncation that was
supposed to replace that empty section did not run, because `grep -n -F "--- 追加："` let the
leading dashes be parsed as options. The duplicate block was then removed by line range and
re-verified, rather than by regenerating the whole archive over an unconfirmed state.
