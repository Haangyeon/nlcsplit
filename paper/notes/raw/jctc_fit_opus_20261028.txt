The core operation—grouping a double sum by fragment pairs—is acknowledged as "trivially additive."

**ADVANCE ASSESSMENT:**
The technical contribution is: (1) explicit retention of pair indices, (2) quantification that fragment-resolved values depend on partition choice with measured ranges of 0.0034-0.3030 kcal/mol on S22, (3) a reporting protocol (use the total for energies; report the partition-family range for fragment descriptors). 

This is *metrological* rather than methodological.
